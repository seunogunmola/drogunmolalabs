How I work

Every build goes round the same five-step loop.

  1. 1

    Notice

    Spot a real problem that real people have.

  2. 2

    Hypothesise

    Make a testable guess about what would help.

  3. 3

    Build

    Make the smallest thing that can test the guess.

  4. 4

    Test

    Put it in front of the people it's for, and measure.

  5. 5

    Learn

    Keep what works, drop what doesn't, go again.
